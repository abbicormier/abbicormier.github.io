How the week works

Each week: read the slide decks, watch the lecture videos, complete the homework. Everything is due Sunday at 11:59 p.m. unless noted. All three exams are taken online through Canvas using Honorlock. No live sessions; office hours are on Zoom, with times and the link in Canvas.

Exam and project dates

These dates are firm and non-negotiable.
  • Pre-project Exercise, Wed Sep 9
  • Exam 1, Tue Sep 29, Honorlock window 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. 60-minute timed exam, begin by 5 p.m.
  • Project Progress Check (optional), Sun Oct 11
  • Exam 2, Tue Nov 17, Honorlock window 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. 60-minute timed exam, begin by 5 p.m.
  • Project final submission, Sun Nov 22
  • Exam 3 and Project Accountability, Fri Dec 18, Honorlock window 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. You get 2 hours, so start by 4 p.m.
Registrar deadlines
  • Add/drop, Fri Sep 4
  • Withdraw from the course, Fri Oct 30
